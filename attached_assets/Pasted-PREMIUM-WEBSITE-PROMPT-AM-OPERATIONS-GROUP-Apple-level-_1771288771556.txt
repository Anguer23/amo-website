PREMIUM WEBSITE PROMPT — “AM OPERATIONS GROUP” (Apple-level, black/white, intentional, alive)

Create a single-page premium website for “AM Operations Group” with the same product-story feel and intentional scroll pacing as Apple.com, with the modern, animated, high-end interaction language of openclaw.ai, but executed in strict black-and-white minimalism. DESIGN SYSTEM / VISUAL LANGUAGE
- Black background (#050505) and white typography with muted gray secondary text.
- Ultra-clean grid, generous spacing, large margins, calm rhythm.
- Use glassy, subtle panels with thin borders (white at ~10–14% opacity).
- Soft shadows and controlled light bloom; no colorful gradients.
- Monospaced labels for metrics (to feel analytical).
- Hero typography: bold, wide, confident (Apple-like).
- Sections should not be crowded. Minimal copy with dense meaning.
- Use intentional scroll storytelling: reveal → pause → deepen → reveal.

MOTION & INTERACTIONS (must feel alive)
- Scrolling reveals: each section fades in + slightly rises with smooth easing.
- The background feels alive: subtle drifting lightfield texture that moves slowly over time.
- Cursor aura: a very subtle soft-light aura follows the cursor (low opacity, non-distracting).
- Hover states: almost everything responds with a new animation—borders brighten, elements lift 1px, micro glows, numbers animate, lines redraw.
- Animations are smooth, controlled, premium—never flashy.
